Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe for children? ↓

Yes! Our bubble solution is 100% non-toxic, hypoallergenic, and environmentally friendly. It's safe for eyes, skin, and even if accidentally swallowed in small amounts.

Do you travel? ↓

We certainly do! We cover a 50-mile radius from our headquarters. For events further away, we apply a modest travel fee per mile.

How long is the show? ↓

Standard shows range from 45 to 90 minutes depending on the package you choose. Custom stage productions can be tailored to any duration.
