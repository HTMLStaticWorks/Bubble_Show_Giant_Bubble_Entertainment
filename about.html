Our Magical Story

Where Science Meets
Artistry

Founded in 2015, Bubble Magic was born from a simple obsession: the physics of surface tension and the pure joy it brings to children and adults alike. We've spent a decade perfecting our secret bubble formula to create the world's most durable and shimmering giant bubbles.

Our Vision

To transform ordinary events into extraordinary memories through the ephemeral beauty of bubbles.

The Science

We utilize STEM principles to teach children about chemistry, physics, and light through interactive play.

Sustainability

Our formula is 100% biodegradable, non-toxic, and safe for all environments and living beings.

Meet the Master Artist

Leading our team is Jonathan "Bubble" Smith, a world-renowned bubble architect with over 15 years of stage experience. His performances have been featured in major festivals, TV shows, and luxury corporate galas across the globe.
